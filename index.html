Put the garment's photo on the figure (rough test)

Add a flat photo of just the garment on a plain background (a flat-lay or ghost-mannequin shot). A photo of a model wearing it will not cut out. The background is removed, then the garment is stretched sideways until it is as wide as the chart says at the bust, waist and hip, and placed on the scan from the shoulder to the hem. Click the sizes under Result to see each one.

Length is counted from

Choose "Top edge" for tube tops, strapless tops and bandeaus: their length runs from the top edge of the garment, which the tool places about 2 in above your bust line.

Hem adjustment (optional)
The real hem sits

Use this when the top sits higher or lower on you than the chart length suggests, for example a stretchy ribbed top that rides up. "Higher" means nearer your head. Leave it empty for no change.

Fit rules (garment measurements)

Room = garment minus body, plus the stretch allowance. Low stretch adds a flat number of inches everywhere, Medium and High add a percentage of the garment's own measurement (so a bigger size gets more), and an elastic or drawstring waist adds the flat number at the waist only. These are starting guesses. Change them when a verdict does not match real life.
